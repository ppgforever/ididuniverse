# IdidUniverse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

IdidUniverse is developed and operated by an individual developer (not a registered company). This Privacy Policy explains what information IdidUniverse ("IdidUniverse," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the IdidUniverse mobile app (the "App"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect your email address, phone number, or real name.** Account identity is based entirely on:

- **A Firebase sign-in identifier (UID)** created when you sign in with Google or Facebook. This is an opaque technical identifier, not your email or name — we never read the email claim Google provides, and we only request `public_profile` (no email) from Facebook.
- **The sign-in provider you used** (Google or Facebook), stored only to show which method you used.
- **A randomly generated 9-character support code**, used solely so you can identify your account to our support team without giving us your email. It is not derived from your identity in any way.

**Content you create:**

- The activity sentences you log in the App (e.g., "I took a walk under the sun").
- Your avatar configuration (character, adjective, and default reaction choices).
- Feedback/reactions you give to other users' activities.

**Automatically collected technical information:**

- Standard sign-in and app diagnostic data collected by Firebase Authentication as part of providing sign-in.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- Operate the core features of the App: saving and displaying your activities, showing you feedback from other users, and showing your activities to others.
- Automatically classify the activities you log using an AI language model, to determine:
  - **Status** (e.g., Positive, Empathy, Negative, Irrelevant, or Inappropriate), which controls whether an activity is shown to other users at all.
  - **Category** (e.g., "Kitchen chore"), used to group similar activities so feedback can be shared across them.
- Screen content for material that isn't suitable for a general audience (explicit sexual content, graphic violence, hate speech, drug glorification, or self-harm content) so it is never shown to other users.
- Respond to support requests, using your support code to locate your account.

We do not use your activity content to build an advertising profile of you, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

- **With other users:** an activity you log is shown to other users only once it is classified as Positive or Empathy, and only alongside your chosen avatar (character + adjective) — never your name, email, or sign-in identifier.
- **Service providers:** we use third-party infrastructure to operate the App, each governed by its own privacy policy:
  - **Google Firebase** — authentication (Google/Facebook sign-in).
  - **Microsoft Azure** — database hosting for your account and activity data.
  - **Meta (Facebook)** — only if you choose to sign in with Facebook.
- We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell personal information.
- We may disclose information if required by law, or to investigate violations of our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html).

## 4. Advertising

The App does not currently show advertising and does not include an advertising SDK, so no advertising identifiers are collected. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy first to describe what the advertising provider collects, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a Microsoft Azure SQL Database with encryption at rest (Transparent Data Encryption) and encrypted in transit (TLS). Every request to our backend is verified against your Firebase sign-in token, and our database is not open to the public internet. No security measure is perfect, but we do not store the kinds of highly sensitive identifiers (email, government ID, payment information) that would make a breach of this data especially harmful.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

You may permanently delete your account at any time from the Settings page (Support → Delete Account). This immediately and permanently deletes:

- Your account record.
- Every activity you've logged.
- Every piece of feedback you've given to others, and every piece of feedback others have given you.
- Your sign-in link and support code.

This action cannot be undone. Note that Firebase/Google and Meta may separately retain their own sign-in records according to their own privacy policies — deleting your IdidUniverse account does not delete your underlying Google or Facebook account.

## 7. Children's Privacy

IdidUniverse is intended for users aged 13 and older. We ask users to confirm they are 13 or older when they sign up, but we do not independently verify age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us (Section 9) and we will delete the associated data.

## 8. Your Choices

- **Access/export:** your activities and feedback are visible to you directly in the App at any time.
- **Deletion:** see Section 6.
- **Support:** contact us using the support code shown on your Settings page, so we can locate your account without needing your email.

## 9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to **ididuniverse@gmail.com**. If you're contacting us about your own account, please include your support code (found on the Settings page) so we can find your records.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
