# How to Delete Your IdidUniverse Data

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

You can delete your IdidUniverse account and all of the data connected to it at any time. You don't need to contact us to do this.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open the IdidUniverse app and sign in.
2. Tap **Settings** in the bottom bar.
3. Tap **Support**, then **Delete account**.
4. Read the message and confirm.

This takes effect right away and cannot be undone. It permanently deletes:

- Your account record.
- Every activity you've logged.
- Every piece of feedback you've given to others, and every piece of feedback others have given you.
- Your sign-in link and support code.

IdidUniverse does not store your email address, so there is no email data to remove.

## If you can't use the app

If you can't sign in, or you've uninstalled the app, email **ididuniverse@gmail.com** from the email address of the Google or Facebook account you used to sign in, and ask us to delete your account. If you still have your support code (shown under **Settings → Support**), include it. We will confirm by reply once your data has been deleted.

## Your Facebook or Google account

Deleting your IdidUniverse account does not delete your Facebook or Google account. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove IdidUniverse from your Facebook settings under **Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites**. Removing the app there stops Facebook from sharing new information with us, but it does not delete the data we already hold, so use the steps above for that.

For more about how we handle your data, see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).
